How to Use Restaurant Business Planner

Getting Started

  1. Start with the Investment tab to enter your startup costs
  2. Move to Premises to set your operational parameters
  3. Review Cash Flow projections based on your inputs
  4. Analyze your business viability in the Results tab

Tips for Success

  • Be realistic with your cost estimates
  • Include a buffer in your working capital
  • Consider seasonal variations in sales
  • Review industry benchmarks for validation

Features

  • Auto-save: Your data is automatically saved locally
  • Export: Generate PDF reports for investors
  • Scenarios: Save multiple versions for comparison
  • Real-time: Calculations update as you type
